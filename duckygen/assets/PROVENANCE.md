# Asset provenance

The vector paths, prop drawings, connectors and synthetic cue waveforms were authored locally for duckygen. They are reusable in generated projects without attribution. They are not copied from the user's reference drawings, product logos, third-party icon libraries or commercial recordings. No claim is made that synthetic cues are recorded foley. index.json records each file's source, tags and content hash.

Manrope medium/bold font files were reused from the installed html-video-explainer package. Their SIL Open Font License is in fonts/OFL.txt and must travel with exported font assets.

Inter, Barlow Condensed, IBM Plex Mono, Fraunces and Caveat were downloaded unmodified from the official `google/fonts` repository at revision `6c1dbe27dcf7d817d4b62cec033ba77fa8e2a5ec` on November 1, 2026. Each family's subfolder contains its upstream OFL.txt. The asset index records the pinned file URL, real weight range and hash. Preserve the selected licenses with redistributed font files; use the original family names. These are third-party open-source fonts, not Duckygen-authored typefaces.

The third-party files under `soundeffects/cc0/` are Kenney Interface Sounds, CC0-1.0. They are not Duckygen-authored effects. The original Kenney license, retrieval provenance and conversion details are documented in `soundeffects/README.md` and `soundeffects/licenses/`. The index retains pinned source URLs and original/converted hashes. Preserve selected license records when copying these assets into a project.

Generic actor and technology artwork in `avatars/` and `illustrations/` incorporates MIT-licensed Tabler icon geometry from https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons at revision `ed177a085b652fe04ec4f37ced867c94621e7840`, retrieved November 1, 2026. Duckygen added original palette-matched organic paper backplates, sketch outlines and accent treatments, then rendered transparent 512px PNG derivatives. Editable derivative SVGs and unmodified upstream SVGs are retained. Preserve `licenses/tabler-icons-MIT.txt`; see `sources/tabler/import-manifest.json` for original filenames, download URLs and hashes. These generic illustrations are not real identities, authentic brand marks or attack evidence.

The measured audio helper and renderer were adapted from the user's installed create-explainer-video and html-video-explainer skills. The new runtime, asset tooling and guidance are project-local work. Source originals were not deleted.

Stylistic inspiration: user-supplied images and prompts, plus https://prompt-motion.com/ (accessed 2026-11-01). The site was consulted as a prompt collection; its third-party videos/assets were not downloaded or included. The example product prompts are not implemented product films, and no absent reference video is claimed to have been studied.

The 16 original prop IDs were refined to the v2 editorial-sketch style with selective fills, consistent stroke hierarchy, flat offset depth and named animation layers. `scripts/build_refined_props.py` is their editable authoring source; verified token/map geometry is preserved under `sources/duckygen-v1/`. Earlier delivered projects keep their own copied assets unchanged.

Fourteen additional hardware props were authored as original Duckygen SVG drawings in `scripts/build_hardware_props.py`, using the same palette, stroke hierarchy and named-layer convention. They depict generic computers, server/network hardware, storage and mobile devices; no product UI, brand geometry or third-party reference image was copied. These originals are reusable in generated projects without required attribution. The index records their hashes, tags, layers, coordinate space and attachment anchors.

The 88 indexed icons received a Duckygen v3 layered-detail pass, authored in `scripts/build_detailed_icons.py`. Selected hardware miniatures derive from Duckygen's original props; other icons retain their original Duckygen or MIT Tabler base geometry with recorded modifications. Source/license records are preserved for retained third-party geometry; original prop-derived replacements are identified as such. Stable asset IDs and project-local copied versions preserve existing delivered projects.

The optional `runtime/vendor/three/` files are unmodified module/core builds from the official `three` npm package, version 0.187.0, with the upstream MIT license and pinned package/file hashes in `provenance.json`. `runtime/three-visualizer.mjs` and `scripts/create_three_demo.py` are original Duckygen integration/example code. The visualization is an illustrative teaching model, not recorded traffic, attack footage or a validated physical/security simulation.

The `icons/tech-*` additions incorporate 24 more MIT-licensed Tabler source images from the same pinned revision, with a Duckygen stroke/palette treatment. The unmodified source SVGs remain under `sources/tabler/`; the index contains their original and modified hashes and source URLs. Preserve the included MIT license when distributing selected assets.

Additional Kenney CC0 sounds cover tactile impacts and computer/sci-fi textures. The original pack license texts are under `soundeffects/licenses/`; per-file entries contain pinned mirror URLs, original file hashes and the actual WAV conversion details. No subjective listening pass is claimed from file decoding or waveform checks.

Five contextual technology photographs were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons with file-specific CC0/public-domain/CC BY 4.0 metadata on November 1, 2026. `sources/wikimedia-photo-records.json` retains the retrieved metadata and per-image credit files live under `licenses/photos/`. The image index records authors, source pages, license terms and hashes. Downloaded thumbnails keep their original colors/content. When using a CC BY photo, include appropriate author/title/source/license credit and note any changes in the finished film's description; do not imply endorsement or that stock context depicts a specific incident.
